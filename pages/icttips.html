ICT Companies in or near Cuijk

Cuijk is not only a great place to live and visit — its also a growing area for technology and innovation. Several companies in and around Cuijk offer interesting opportunities for ICT professionals and software developers. Here are three examples:

  • 1. Ivido, Location: Cuijk, About: Ivido develops secure digital platforms for healthcare. Their software helps patients and doctors share medical data safely and easily. The company focuses on innovation, privacy, and user-friendly design — great for developers interested in web applications and health tech.
  • 2. Axians Location: Nijmegen (near Cuijk) About: Axians is a large international ICT company that offers services in networking, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and data analytics. Many developers from the Cuijk area work here. Its a good example of a company where you can work with modern technologies and large-scale projects.
  • 3. Senta ICT Location: Boxmeer (10 minutes from Cuijk) About: Senta ICT provides IT infrastructure, cloud solutions, and technical support for local businesses. Its a smaller company with a friendly atmosphere, ideal for ICT students who want to gain experience in different parts of the field — from hardware to software.
